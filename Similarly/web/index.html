FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Similarly.

Are my photos uploaded to any external server?

Never. Similarly runs 100% locally on your iPhone using Apple's Neural Engine and Vision framework. No photo, thumbnail, or metadata ever leaves your device.

Can I recover accidentally deleted photos?

Yes! Deleted photos are moved to the standard iOS 'Recently Deleted' album in the Photos app, where you can restore them within 30 days.

Does Similarly require an internet connection?

No, Similarly works completely offline and even in Airplane Mode.

How does it detect similar photos?

Similarly uses Vision FeaturePrint vector analysis, capturing visual patterns, textures, and compositions rather than simple file hashes.
