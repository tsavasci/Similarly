Your privacy matters to us

This Privacy Policy describes our data processing practices to protect the privacy of users of the Similarly application ("App"). By using our App, you agree to the data collection and use practices described in this policy.

🛡️ Important Note: Similarly is a privacy-first application. All photo processing happens on your device, and your photos are never sent to our servers.

1. Information We Collect

The Similarly application may collect the following types of information:

  • Device Information: Technical information such as device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and crash data.
  • Usage Data: Your in-app interactions, feature usage, and preferences.
  • Photos: We may access photos on your device only with your explicit permission and solely for similarity analysis purposes.

2. How We Use Data

We may use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide the App's core functionality
  • To improve user experience
  • To analyze App performance and fix bugs
  • To develop new features
  • To provide technical support
  • To fulfill our legal obligations

3. Data Security

The security of your data is our highest priority:

  • Local Processing: All data processing occurs only on your device.
  • No Servers: Your photos and personal data are never sent to our servers or shared with third parties.
  • Stays on Device: The App uses only your device's processing power to analyze photos.
  • Encryption: All sensitive data is stored encrypted.

💡 Security Commitment: Similarly only accesses your photos for similarity analysis and never sends this data externally. All processing is 100% local.

4. Third-Party Services

Our App may use the following third-party services to improve our service quality:

  • Crash Reporting: Used to detect and fix App crashes. Only technical data is collected, your photos are not included.
  • Analytics Tools: Used to analyze user interactions anonymously. Your personal information is not shared.

5. Data Retention

We retain collected data only for the purposes specified in this Privacy Policy and in accordance with applicable laws. Your data will be kept for as long as necessary for the purposes outlined in this policy.

  • Usage data: Maximum 12 months
  • Crash reports: Maximum 90 days
  • Account information: Until your account is deleted

6. User Rights

Under GDPR and similar regulations, you have the following rights:

  • Request access to your data
  • Request correction of your data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to data processing
  • Request data portability

7. Children's Privacy

Our App is not designed for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you learn that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

8. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will notify you by posting the new privacy policy on this page. For significant changes, in-app notifications may be sent.

We encourage you to review this page periodically. Changes become effective after they are posted on this page.

  • GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) - European Union
  • CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act) - California, USA
  • KVKK (Personal Data Protection Law) - Turkey
